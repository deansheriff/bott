## Payment

We accept bank transfer, card payment and cash on pickup. We never ask for your card PIN or OTP in chat.

## Ordering

Orders are confirmed by a team member. The bot can answer questions but cannot place or confirm an order.

## Allergies

If you have a food allergy, tell us before ordering. A team member will check ingredients with the kitchen. The bot cannot give allergy advice.

## Refunds and complaints

Refunds and complaints are handled by a team member. Send your order reference and a short description, and we will get back to you.
